# Development placeholders — not production assets

These are the placeholder people and photos from the approved design boards
(`design/BOARDS.md`: "People and photos in the boards are placeholders"). They exist so the
Welcome collage and the invite faces look like the boards during development.

They must not ship to testers or the stores. The production source for the Welcome collage
and the invite faces is an open decision for Nemat (see `docs/PHASE-2-REPORT.md`, section 9).
All references go through `src/ui/devPlaceholders.ts`.
