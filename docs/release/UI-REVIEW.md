## Needs a design (one list)

Each is something the app does today with no approved board, or a decision the boards cannot make. None was changed.

1. **A failure message and a way to retry, for actions and loads.** About 70 places log an error and show nothing: the Home feed failing (a blank black screen), a delete that fails (the sheet just closes), report, block, follow, comment send, request a review, profile and place loads. One reusable pattern would cover all of them (an inline line with Try again, or a short toast). The canvas note already lists "not designed yet" states; this one is not on it.
2. **Video reply variants of existing boards.** Delete ("Delete your reply?" / "Delete reply" is in the app; SPEC.md says "same confirmation", whose body, "Video replies people sent stay on their own profiles", is wrong for a video reply, which instead leaves replies to it showing "Replying to a deleted video"); the report and block sheets ("Why are you reporting this response?", "You won't see this response again.") for a video reply; the unavailable screen for a video reply ("This response isn't available"); Activity's "liked your response" for a like on a video reply. The decision of 14 Oct ("video reply" for replies) covers the failed-post states only.
3. **A profile that cannot be shown** (missing, blocked, failed to load): today a blank page. Also the video reply player's unavailable state has no Next or Try again (it is not the X-Unavailable board).
4. **Home: a video that fails to load in the feed.** X-Unavailable is a full screen; whether Home shows it over the conversation or something quieter is a design call. A buffering indicator for slow connections (Bali mobile data) has no board either.
5. **Recent searches belong to the phone, not the account.** SR-Recent says "stay on this phone only". Someone signing in as a different account on the same phone sees the previous account's searches. Decide: keep (as drawn), clear on sign-out, or keep per account.
6. **Largest text sizes.** The screens are laid out at fixed positions for an 844 pt screen. Decide the largest supported text size (a cap of about 130 percent is the usual answer) or draw the large-text layout. The cap needs a text wrapper component in every screen, not a setting.
7. **Contrast tokens**: `textTertiary` and `dangerBorder` are under 4.5:1 for small text. Darker values (for example #6E6E73 for secondary text, #D70015 for destructive text, both already tokens) would pass; this is a change to the boards' colours, so it is yours to approve.
8. **Draft banner copy** ("Saving your draft", "Your draft didn't save") and **"Ask someone"'s behaviour** (share sheet with the prompt link) need approving or replacing.
9. **P6 for a first-response draft** (see `design/proposals/FAILED-POST-REVIEW.md`).
