## 2. Who can use Conga

- You must be **18 or older**. When you sign up we ask "Are you 18 or older?"; if you say no you are signed out and
  no profile is created. We do not check ID. If we find that an account belongs to someone under 18, we remove it.
- Conga is in a **closed beta**. To make a profile you need a valid invite from a member, or admission from the
  waitlist. Invites are single-use and expire (7 days at the time of writing, a setting we can change). Access can be
  limited, paused or ended during the beta.
- You may have one account, and one sign-in method per account: Apple, Google or an email code. There are no
  passwords. Accounts cannot be merged. You are responsible for what happens under your account and for keeping
  your sign-in secure.

## 3. Your account and profile

You choose a name, a username and optionally a bio and photo. They must not impersonate anyone, break the Community
Guidelines or mislead people. We may change or remove a name, username or photo that does.

## 4. Videos, comments and everything you make

- **What you can make.** Responses (videos up to 30 seconds made for a prompt), video replies (videos in reply to a
  response or another video reply), captions up to 150 characters, text comments up to 300 characters, and prompt
  suggestions. Videos are recorded inside the app; you cannot upload from your camera roll.
- **They are public.** Every response and video reply is public. Anyone on Conga can watch it, people can reply on
  video and share it, and anyone with a link can watch it in a browser without an account. The app tells you this
  and asks you to accept before your first public video ("Your videos are public").
- **You own your content; we need permission to run the service.** [LEGAL REVIEW: write the licence you give us to

- **Prompts you suggest.** If we approve one it becomes a public prompt, with your name unless you turn that off.
  We may decline a suggestion and we do not reword them.
- **Deleting.** You can delete your videos and comments whenever you like. Deleted content disappears everywhere at
  once, including shared web links. There is no undo once you confirm. A video that other people replied to
  leaves an empty "deleted" placeholder so their replies make sense. [LEGAL REVIEW: copies already shared by others,

People on our team review reports and decide case by case; nothing is automatic. We may remove content, warn you,
pause your ability to respond, reply, comment or suggest prompts until a date, or suspend your account. Severe
cases can skip steps. A notice tells you what happened and why, and you can **request a review** of a removal,
restriction or suspension, once per decision. We do not promise how quickly we respond. Details are in
`MODERATION-POLICY.md`. [LEGAL REVIEW: confirm we are entitled to act and keep content as described, and the notice
