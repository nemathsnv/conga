## What to use for what

| I want to... | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Report a video, comment or person | Use the in-app report (share sheet, the ... menu on a profile, or long-press a comment). It is anonymous. This is faster than writing to us. |
| Stop seeing someone | Block them (Settings > Blocked accounts to undo). |
| Challenge a removal, restriction or suspension | Open the notice and choose **Request a review**. |
| Delete my account | Settings > Delete account (`ACCOUNT-DELETION.md`). |

## What to expect

- Moderation and support during the beta are handled by the founder and a small team.
- We do not promise how quickly we reply.
- We will not ask for a password (there are none), a sign-in code, or payment. Anyone asking for those is not us.

## For the team: what must exist before the beta

1. A real address or form, monitored by a named person, with an auto-reply that carries no time promise.
2. The Settings > Help and feedback row opens it (currently inactive; wiring is a small change once a URL exists).
3. This page, the Privacy Policy, Terms and Community Guidelines hosted at public URLs (store listing support URL, and
   the Apple and Google policy links). The in-app place for the Privacy Policy and Terms links has no approved board.
4. A rehearsed way to verify a person who lost their sign-in, written down so every team member does it the same way.
5. A defined process for legal and copyright notices.
