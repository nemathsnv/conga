## What Conga is for

Conga is where people respond to prompts with short videos (up to 30 seconds) and talk back with video replies.
It works when people feel safe being honest. These guidelines say what is not allowed, how you can report it, and
what we do. They apply to everything you make or send on Conga: responses, video replies, captions, comments,
your name, username, bio and photo, and the prompts you suggest.

Conga is for people who are **18 or older**. It is in a **closed beta**, joined by invite or from the waitlist.

## Your videos are public

Every response and video reply is public. Anyone on Conga can watch it, people can reply on video and share it,
and anyone with a link can watch it in a browser without an account. Think about that before you record. You can
delete any of your videos at any time.

## Prompts you suggest

A person on our team reads every suggested prompt before anyone else sees it, and we do not reword prompts; we
publish or decline them. Prompts that will not be published: ones that single out a person or a group, ones too
similar to a live prompt, promotion, ones that ask for private details, ones that are unclear, and other reasons we
will tell you. You can rewrite and send a declined prompt again.

## Comments

Comments are text only, up to 300 characters, with no links or images. You can delete your own. A response's author
cannot delete other people's comments, but can report them.

## Reporting and blocking

- **Report** from the share sheet on any response or video reply, the ... menu on a profile, or by long-pressing a
  comment. Pick a reason. **Reports are anonymous: the person you report is not told who reported them.**
- **Block** a person if you do not want to see them. It is silent: they are not told. You both stop seeing each
  other's profiles, videos, replies and comments, you stop getting notifications about each other, and any follows
  between you end. Unblock in Settings > Blocked accounts.
- Reporting is how we find out. We do not scan for problems automatically in the beta [PLACEHOLDER: confirm: the
  decision on automated checks of uploads is still open in `SPEC.md` section 22], and people review every report.

## What we do

A person on our team reviews each report, case by case. There are no automatic penalties and we do not promise how
fast we will answer. [PLACEHOLDER: confirm that this is the public wording Nemat wants.]

- **First ordinary violation:** we remove the content and you get a warning. The notice says what was removed and why,
  and: "Nobody can watch it any more. Please treat this as a warning."
- **A repeated similar violation:** posting is paused until a date you are shown. You can still watch, but you cannot
  respond, reply, comment or suggest prompts until then.
- **Further, repeated or severe abuse:** we suspend the account. You cannot use Conga with it; at sign-in you see why.
- **Severe cases can skip steps.**
- **Nothing in a notice promises a next step.** A notice never says an automatic penalty will follow.

## If you think we got it wrong

On a removal, a restriction or a suspension, choose **Request a review**: "We'll review it again." You can ask for one
review of each decision. A different person on our team looks again [PLACEHOLDER: confirm "a different person"; the
spec does not say who reviews]. We send you the result as a notification, in one of two ways:

- "We reviewed your request. The decision stands."
- "We reviewed your request and reversed the decision."
